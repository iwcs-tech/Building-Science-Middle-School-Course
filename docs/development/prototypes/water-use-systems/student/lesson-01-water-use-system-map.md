## Important Boundary

Learning Studio Wren, its map, and all notes below are **fictional instructional examples**. This task helps you make a careful model from supplied evidence. It does not ask you to inspect, diagnose, or recommend changes to a real building.

## Vocabulary

- **input:** something that enters a system
- **use:** a purpose or process that needs a resource in the system
- **output:** something that leaves a system or moves beyond its boundary
- **potable water:** water supplied for uses such as drinking and handwashing in this fictional model
- **wastewater:** used water that leaves a stated system through a drain in this fictional model
- **unknown:** the supplied evidence does not tell us enough to make a claim

2. The map names handwashing sinks and a small learning-kitchen sink as uses with drains.
3. The cooling-loop model gives off water vapor outdoors; the map does not show its internal equipment.
4. The courtyard garden uses water; some water enters soil and some changes to water vapor.
5. The roof has no drainage information. Do not add a roof path without naming it as unknown.
