## Fictional Design Brief

Learning Studio Wren has two possible **model forecasts** for the same fictional school day. A class wants to decide which model better fits these stated priorities:

1. Provide the named handwashing, learning-kitchen, cooling, and garden uses.
2. Keep the modeled total potable-water input at or below **1,000 water units**.
3. Keep the modeled garden use at or below **150 water units**.

**Constraint:** The class has only the information on this page. It cannot assume that the two days had the same weather, attendance, or equipment conditions.
