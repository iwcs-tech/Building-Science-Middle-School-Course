# Residential Water-Use Reasoning Rubric

_Formative draft for the two student pages; not a course-level assessment decision._

| Criterion | Emerging | Developing | Strong evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| System paths | Some parts named but arrows or boundary are unclear | Traces input, at least one use, and output | Accurately distinguishes cold, hot, indoor drain, and outdoor path within a clear boundary |
| Evidence and uncertainty | Adds unsupported paths or omits evidence | Supports key arrows and notes one unknown | Supports paths with case details and resists filling evidence gaps |
| Model calculation | Comparison is missing or incorrect | Computes 18 L, 6 L, and 12 L with support | Computes and labels all values and states they are invented model outputs |
| Qualified explanation | Treats the number as a real-home guarantee | Makes a model-limited claim and names a missing factor | Uses the comparison accurately, identifies a relevant tradeoff, and explains why energy/real design cannot be concluded |

Feedback should target reasoning rather than a preferred household design. A student may show understanding through drawing, writing, typing, or oral explanation.
