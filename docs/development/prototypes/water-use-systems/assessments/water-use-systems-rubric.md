# Water-Use Systems Reasoning Rubric

_Draft prototype assessment tool._

Use this rubric for the Lesson 1 system map and Lesson 2 explanation. Base the final score on Lesson 2 while using Lesson 1 to inform feedback about system boundaries and evidence.

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Secure | Extending |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **System-map evidence** | Leaves the water-use system incomplete or adds unsupported routes. | Identifies one input, use, or output but does not connect it clearly to evidence. | Traces a plausible input → use → output path and identifies an unknown using supplied evidence. | Compares more than one plausible path and explains what evidence would distinguish them. |
| **Key distinctions** | Treats water use and enclosure moisture movement as the same idea. | Names the ideas but applies one inaccurately or without evidence. | Correctly distinguishes a stated water-use path from enclosure moisture movement in the fictional case. | Explains how the ideas can connect without treating either model as complete. |
| **Comparison with criteria** | Chooses a model without using the stated priorities or constraint. | Cites one output but does not compare models clearly. | Uses at least two supplied outputs to compare the fictional models against the priorities and constraint. | Weighs several criteria and explains why reasonable readers could prioritize them differently. |
| **Tradeoff and limitation** | Gives no tradeoff, limitation, or needed information. | Names one without explaining why it matters. | States a relevant tradeoff and a limitation or additional information needed. | Explains how added evidence could qualify or revise the claim. |
| **Communication and boundary** | Response is incomplete, unclear, or treats the case as a real-building judgment. | Claim is present but needs support with vocabulary, evidence, or the fictional boundary. | Clear claim connects evidence, comparison, tradeoff, and the fictional-case boundary. | Uses precise language and constructively responds to a peer question about uncertainty. |

## Student-Friendly Success Criteria

Before submitting, I can check that I:

- labeled a water path with evidence;
- separated water use from enclosure moisture movement;
- compared supplied model outputs against the stated priorities;
- named a tradeoff; and
- stated a limitation or question without making a claim about a real building.
