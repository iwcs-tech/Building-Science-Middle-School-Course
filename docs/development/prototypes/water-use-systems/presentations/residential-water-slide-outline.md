# Residential Water-Use Slide Outline

_Markdown source for a possible teacher display; no external visual assets._

| Slide | Content / visual to create | Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Title and outline of fictional Home Cedar | What is inside our system boundary? |
| 2 | Case-card text with supply and service pipe highlighted | Which arrow is supported? |
| 3 | Blank text-arrow map with cold branch, heater, fixtures, drains, garden | Where can each path be traced? |
| 4 | Two labeled examples: planned fixture water use; hypothetical roof moisture | Which is in the case evidence? |
| 5 | Heater → hot pipe → shower → drain diagram | What happens while a person waits for warm water? |
| 6 | Invented 6 L versus 2 L per event table, clearly marked “model only” | What stays the same? |
| 7 | Three-event arithmetic: 18 L, 6 L, difference 12 L | What does this number describe? |
| 8 | Missing-evidence board: flow, pipe size, temperature, energy, construction cost, feasibility | What cannot we decide from this model? |

Use words, arrows, and high-contrast labels, not color alone. Any later images require rights and accessibility review.
