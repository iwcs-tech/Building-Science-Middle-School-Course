# Teacher Plan 2: Compare Hot-Water Paths

_Review-only draft; 40 minutes; grades 7–8._

## Prepare

Distribute the [comparison page](../student/lesson-02-hot-water-path-comparison.md) and display slides 5–8 of the [outline](../presentations/residential-water-slide-outline.md). Explain that the 6 L and 2 L values are invented instructional outputs. No water-heater demonstration or plumbing access is needed.

## Learning target

Students compare two simplified hot-water paths, calculate modeled wait-water differences, and state why that one measure cannot decide a real home's design.

| Time | Facilitation move | Student evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–6 min | Revisit water heater → hot pipe → shower → drain. Ask what “waiting for hot water” means; distinguish it from water used during the shower. | Path sketch |
| 6–11 min | Read the table and its assumptions. Model 1 event for each layout without making a real-world recommendation. | Annotated table |
| 11–24 min | Students draw paths and calculate 3 × 6 = 18 L, 3 × 2 = 6 L, and 18 − 6 = 12 L. Check units and the comparison. | Calculations |
| 24–33 min | Partners draft a claim for the invented model and list missing information: pipe size, flow, actual use, construction cost, feasibility, or temperature/energy data. | Qualified claim and limits |
| 33–38 min | Discuss why a smaller modeled wait-water amount does not itself quantify energy savings or establish that changing a real house is worthwhile. | Revised explanation |
| 38–40 min | Collect the student page and one remaining question. | Formative check |

## Teacher check

The expected arithmetic is 18 L versus 6 L, a modeled difference of 12 L over three events. Accept other accurate explanations of the limitation. Do not equate all wait-water with hot water or infer energy saved from the table: its only measured variable is invented volume to drain. DOE's [core plumbing layout explanation](https://bsesc.energy.gov/energy-basics/basics-core-plumbing-layout) supports the general relation between layout and wait, not these figures or a retrofit plan.

## Access and safety

Provide multiplication scaffolds or a two-column tally, and allow oral claims. Do not request household examples, water bills, or plumbing photographs. There is no actual water testing, diagnosis, repair, or purchasing recommendation.
