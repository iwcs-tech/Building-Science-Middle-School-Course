# Lesson 2 Student Page: Control-Plan Comparison

## Studio Meridian: Choose Within a Fictional Model

Everything on this page is invented for learning. The plans do not describe the school or tell anyone how to run a real building.

### Stated Priorities

1. Keep the modeled temperature between 21 and 24 C for as much of the class period as possible.
2. Keep at least 70 daylight points for the modeled class period.
3. Use no more than 10 model-energy points.

### Constraints

- The model cannot move the window.
- The model may use only one shade-response card per time block.
- The model may not use more than 10 model-energy points.

### Fictional Plans and Outputs

| Plan | Decision rule | Temperature in target band | Daylight points | Model-energy points |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: Fixed Schedule | At 10:00, move the shade to “medium” and keep it there. | 44 of 50 minutes | 82 | 8 |
| B: Signal Rule | If the dashboard shows more than 24 C, move the shade to “more shade”; if it shows 21–24 C, keep the current response. | 47 of 50 minutes | 71 | 10 |
