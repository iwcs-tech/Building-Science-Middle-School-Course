# Climate-Context Reasoning Rubric

_Draft prototype assessment tool._

Use this rubric for the Lesson 1 climate-context evidence map and the Lesson 2 explanation. The final score should be based on Lesson 2 and informed by the earlier work.

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Secure | Extending |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Climate evidence** | Does not identify a relevant pattern or confuses the fictional cards. | Identifies one relevant clue but needs support connecting it to the context. | Uses temperature and/or precipitation evidence accurately to describe a supplied context. | Connects several clues and distinguishes climate patterns from a single weather event. |
| **System question** | Gives a repair recommendation or an unrelated statement. | Names a broad building topic without connecting it to evidence. | Uses evidence to pose a relevant building-system question. | Explains why several system questions could be reasonable and identifies a boundary. |
| **Context-based comparison** | Chooses an option without comparing data or contexts. | Uses one data point but does not compare both options clearly. | Uses at least two supplied outputs to compare options in the stated context. | Explains why a result can differ by context and weighs competing criteria. |
| **Tradeoff and limitation** | Does not identify a tradeoff, limitation, or needed evidence. | Names one without explaining its importance. | States a relevant tradeoff and a limitation or additional evidence needed. | Explains how more evidence could test or qualify the fictional model. |
| **Communication** | Response is incomplete or hard to follow. | Claim is present but vocabulary or reasoning needs support. | Clear claim connects context, evidence, comparison, and tradeoff. | Precise explanation responds constructively to a peer question or critique. |

## Student-Friendly Success Criteria

Before submitting, I can check that I:

- used a clue from the climate card;
- asked or answered a building-system question without diagnosing a real building;
- compared data from the correct context;
- named a tradeoff; and
- stated a limitation or question I still have.
