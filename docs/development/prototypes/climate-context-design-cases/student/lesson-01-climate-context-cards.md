## Important Boundary

The two places and all data below are **fictional instructional examples**. A climate pattern can help us ask a building question, but it does not diagnose a real building or tell us what to build.

| --- | --- |

## Climate Card B: Sun Mesa

| Long-term pattern | Fictional climate-card evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Cool season | Days are usually mild; nights can be cool. |
| Warm season | Many afternoons are above 90°F. |
| Precipitation | Long dry stretches are common; rain often arrives in short bursts. |
