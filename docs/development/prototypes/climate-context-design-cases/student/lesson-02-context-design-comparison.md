## Design Brief

Two fictional communities are planning the same small reading room. Both have a limited budget of **3 credits**. The team is comparing two choices for the south-facing window area.

The table contains **fictional model outputs**. It does not predict a real building, recommend a product, or give a repair estimate.

## Criteria and Constraint

The design should:

- lower the modeled indoor heat-stress index;
- keep useful daylight when possible;
- lower the modeled seasonal energy-demand index; and
- cost no more than 3 credits.

Lower heat-stress and energy-demand indexes are better. Higher daylight ratings are better.
