# Moisture Pathways Rubric

_Draft prototype assessment tool._

Use this rubric for the Lesson 1 model and the Lesson 2 final explanation. The final evaluation should emphasize Lesson 2 while using Lesson 1 to identify developing ideas.

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Secure | Extending |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Observation and inference** | Does not distinguish case details from an explanation. | Identifies one observation or inference but mixes them together. | Separates stated observations from a plausible inference. | Uses several observations precisely and explains how they constrain the model. |
| **Source → path → effect model** | Model is missing or its parts are unrelated to the case. | Identifies a source, path, or effect but the connection needs support. | Connects a plausible source, path, and effect using the case evidence. | Compares two possible models or identifies where the path remains uncertain. |
| **Moisture-pathway reasoning** | Label is absent or inconsistent with the simplified case. | Chooses a plausible label but gives limited evidence. | Correctly applies a simplified pathway label and cites at least two relevant details. | Explains why another pathway is less supported by the supplied evidence or could also occur in a fuller system. |
| **Limitation and additional evidence** | Treats the model as certain or proposes an unrelated question. | Names a general limitation with little connection to the claim. | States one meaningful unknown or additional evidence that would strengthen the claim. | Explains how the additional evidence could distinguish between competing models. |
| **Communication** | Explanation is incomplete or difficult to follow. | Includes a claim but needs clearer vocabulary or reasoning. | Clearly connects claim, evidence, source → path → effect, and limitation. | Uses precise vocabulary and responds constructively to a peer's question or critique. |

## Suggested Use

- Do not grade the Lesson 1 model for professional accuracy; give feedback on evidence and uncertainty.
- For Lesson 2, mark one reasoning strength and one specific next step.
- A student may earn a secure score with a different plausible model if it accurately uses the provided evidence and limitation.

## Student-Friendly Success Criteria

Before submitting, I can check that I:

- separated what I observed from what I inferred;
- showed a source, a path, and an effect;
- used at least two details as evidence;
- named a moisture pathway; and
- explained one limitation or missing piece of information.
