# Air and Ventilation Systems Prototype

_Draft instructional prototype for educator review. It is not yet tested, classroom-reviewed curriculum._

This two-meeting sequence introduces a bounded air-systems routine through fictional evidence. Seventh- and eighth-grade students distinguish planned ventilation, filtration, and unplanned air movement, then compare supplied choices using stated criteria and constraints.

## Review Before Use

- All rooms, diagrams, data, choices, and outputs are fictional instructional materials. They do not describe the school or a real HVAC system.
- Confirm timing, vocabulary, reading level, and print/digital formats with intended students before classroom use.
- Use the teacher plans as adaptable facilitation notes, not word-for-word scripts.
- Do not ask students to inspect the actual school, enter mechanical spaces, collect health or symptom information, interpret codes, operate equipment, or recommend repairs.

## Source and Asset Status

The science background draws on the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency's guidance that school HVAC systems support ventilation, filtration, thermal comfort, moisture control, and other building-performance goals. The fictional cases, diagrams, data displays, and prompts are original instructional text. No external visual assets are included.

- [EPA: Heating, Ventilation and Air-Conditioning Systems](https://www.epa.gov/iaq-schools/heating-ventilation-and-air-conditioning-systems-part-indoor-air-quality-design-tools)
