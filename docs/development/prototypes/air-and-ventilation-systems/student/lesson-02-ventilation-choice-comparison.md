## Fictional Design Brief: Learning Studio Kestrel

The class wants to compare two **fictional** plans for a warm afternoon. The plans will be compared only against these stated priorities:

1. Bring some outdoor air into the studio.
2. Reduce the number of particles in air that travels through a filter path.
3. Keep the modeled noise level low enough for a small-group discussion.
4. Stay within an energy budget of **4 model units**.

These are learning criteria, not real HVAC requirements.

### How to Read the Models

- More arrows = more modeled outdoor-air exchange.
- More filter dots = more air moving through the fictional filter path.
- More noise dots = more modeled outside noise.
- The values are not measurements and do not predict a real room.
