## Important Boundary

Learning Studio Kestrel, its diagram, and all notes below are **fictional instructional examples**. This task helps you make a careful model from supplied evidence. It does not ask you to inspect, diagnose, or recommend changes to a real building.

## Vocabulary

- **planned ventilation:** a planned exchange of indoor and outdoor air
- **filtration:** air passes through material that captures some particles
- **unplanned air movement:** air moves through an unintended route
- **unknown:** the supplied evidence does not tell us enough to make a claim

### Supplied Evidence Notes

1. The diagram labels the outdoor-air intake as open.
2. Air on the marked intake path passes through the filter box before reaching the supply vent.
3. The return grille is labeled as a route carrying air away from the studio.
4. The window is closed during the fictional lesson.
5. A dotted mark near the wall seam is shown only to remind us that the diagram does not provide enough information about every possible route.
