# Materials Tradeoff Reasoning Rubric

_Draft prototype assessment tool._

Use this rubric for the Lesson 1 journey map and Lesson 2 explanation. The final score should be based on Lesson 2 and informed by the earlier work.

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Secure | Extending |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Life-cycle model** | Stages are missing, out of sequence, or unrelated to the scenario. | Identifies some stages but needs support sequencing or explaining them. | Correctly sequences the simplified stages and explains why more than one can matter. | Shows connections or a possible reuse loop while naming a model limitation. |
| **Stage-based evidence** | Provides no relevant evidence or treats a rating as a universal fact. | Uses one rating or stage but does not compare options clearly. | Uses supplied evidence from at least two stages to compare both options. | Weighs evidence from several stages and explains why one priority matters in the stated scenario. |
| **Criteria and constraint** | Chooses an option without referring to stated goals or cost. | Refers to one criterion or cost but comparison is incomplete. | Makes a defensible choice using the stated criteria and the three-credit constraint. | Explains how changing a criterion or constraint could reasonably change the choice. |
| **Tradeoff and limitation** | Does not identify a tradeoff, limitation, or needed evidence. | Names one without explaining its importance. | States one relevant tradeoff and a limitation or additional evidence needed. | Explains how additional evidence could revise or qualify the fictional model. |
| **Communication** | Response is incomplete or difficult to follow. | Claim is present but needs clearer vocabulary or reasoning. | Clear explanation connects claim, stage-based evidence, criteria, and tradeoff. | Precise explanation responds constructively to a peer question or critique. |

## Student-Friendly Success Criteria

Before submitting, I can check that I:

- used more than one life-cycle stage;
- compared both fictional options;
- used the stated criteria and cost constraint;
- named a tradeoff; and
- explained one limitation or question I still have.
