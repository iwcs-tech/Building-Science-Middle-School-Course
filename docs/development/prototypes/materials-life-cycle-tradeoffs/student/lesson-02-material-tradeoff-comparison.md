## Design Brief

A fictional library is choosing an interior wall-panel option for a small reading nook. Both options meet the basic size requirement and cost **no more than 3 credits**.

The table contains **fictional instructional ratings**. It does not compare real products, predict real impacts, or recommend a purchase.

## Criteria and Constraint

The team wants to:

- reduce the fictional resource-input rating when possible;
- consider manufacture, use, and end-of-use stages;
- choose a panel that can serve the reading nook for a long time; and
- stay within 3 credits.

For resource input and manufacture impact, lower ratings are better. For durability and end-of-use options, higher ratings are better.
