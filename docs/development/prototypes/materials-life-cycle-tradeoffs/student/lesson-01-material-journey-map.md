## Important Boundary

The components and statements below are **fictional instructional examples**. They help us ask questions about a material life cycle. They do not describe a real product or tell us what to buy or install.

## Four Simplified Stages

1. **Resource acquisition** — obtaining raw materials
2. **Manufacture** — making a usable product
3. **Use and maintenance** — using and caring for the product
4. **End-of-use management** — reusing, recycling, or discarding it after removal
