# Materials Life-Cycle Tradeoffs Prototype

_Draft instructional prototype for educator review. It is not yet tested, classroom-reviewed curriculum._

This two-meeting sequence asks seventh- and eighth-grade students to trace a material through a simplified life cycle, then compare fictional component options using supplied evidence. It tests whether materials and environmental impact should be a recurring course context.

## Review Before Use

- All components, ratings, costs, and scenario details are fictional instructional data. They do not compare real products or give purchasing, construction, or repair advice.
- Confirm timing, reading level, vocabulary, and print/digital formats with the intended learners before classroom use.
- Use the teacher plans as adaptable facilitation notes, not word-for-word scripts.
- Students compare supplied information only. They do not evaluate actual school materials, make procurement decisions, or interpret safety or building standards.

## Source and Asset Status

The science background draws on the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency's [Sustainable Materials Management Basics](https://www.epa.gov/smm/sustainable-materials-management-basics), which describes material life-cycle stages and their environmental impacts. The components, ratings, diagrams, data tables, and prompts in this prototype are original instructional text. No external visual assets are included.
