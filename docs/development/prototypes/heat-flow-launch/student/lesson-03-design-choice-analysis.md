## Design Brief

Classroom Alder is a **fictional** room with a desk that becomes hot in afternoon sunlight. A planning team wants to reduce the modeled heat gain while considering daylight, cooling demand, and a limited budget.

The table gives **modeled instructional data**, not real measurements, product claims, or repair estimates.

## Criteria and Constraint

Your recommendation should:

- lower the modeled desk-surface temperature;
- keep as much daylight as possible;
- lower the modeled cooling-demand index; and
- cost no more than **3 budget credits**.

Lower desk temperatures and cooling-demand indexes are better. Higher daylight ratings are better.
