## Review Before Use

- All room names, temperatures, ratings, and design-option results are fictional instructional data. They do not describe the school or recommend any school repair.
- Confirm that timing, reading level, vocabulary, and print/digital formats work with the intended students before classroom use.
- Use the teacher plans as facilitation notes, not word-for-word scripts. Adapt examples and supports for the local setting.
- Do not collect health or symptom information, enter mechanical spaces, or ask students to diagnose building problems.

## Source and Asset Status

The science background draws on the U.S. Department of Energy's [Heat Flow — Building Science Education](https://bsesc.energy.gov/energy-basics/heat-flow). The scenario, diagrams, data displays, cards, and prompts in this prototype are original instructional text. No external visual assets are included.
