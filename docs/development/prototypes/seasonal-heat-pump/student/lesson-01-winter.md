# Lesson 1: Winter Heat Journey

## Fictional case

Home Finch is an invented single-family home. On a winter day, its indoor space is warmer than the outdoor air. A powered air-source heat pump runs in heating mode.

**Evidence card:** The system takes heat from outdoor air and releases heat indoors. Electrical energy powers the equipment. Its refrigeration cycle enables this overall transfer even though the outdoor air is colder than the room. The system does not need to bring outdoor air into the room to transfer heat.

1. Before using the card, sketch your first idea of how the home gets heat. Label what you are unsure about.
2. Draw three boxes: outdoor air, heat-pump system, indoor space. Draw and label a heat-transfer arrow and a separate electrical-energy input. Label the system boundary.
3. Revise your first model. Explain the heat journey in two or three sentences and cite one detail from the card.
4. What does your drawing leave out? Why should someone not use it to predict a particular home's energy bill?

You may use words, arrows, or a spoken explanation. The case is invented; no information about your own home is needed.
