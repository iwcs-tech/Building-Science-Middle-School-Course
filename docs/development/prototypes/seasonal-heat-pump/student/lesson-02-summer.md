# Lesson 2: One System, Two Seasons

## Fictional evidence card

In summer, Home Finch's heat pump runs in cooling mode. It takes heat from the indoor space and releases heat outdoors. Electrical energy powers the system. The diagram is a simplified energy model, not a picture of outdoor air entering the home.

1. Draw indoor space, heat-pump system, and outdoor air. Label the summer heat-transfer direction and electrical input. Compare your sketch with winter: what reverses, and what input remains?
2. Use this invented accounting model. Every unit is the same amount of energy; it is not a real measurement.

| Mode | Heat taken in | Electrical energy in | Heat delivered or released |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Winter | 3 units from outdoors | 1 unit | 4 units indoors |
| Summer | 3 units from indoors | 1 unit | 4 units outdoors |

Explain where the four units in each row come from. Does this example tell you the cost or performance of a real home? Why?

3. Respond to this claim: “The heat pump makes cold and sends it outside in summer.” State what moves, where it moves, and which card or model evidence supports your answer.
4. Name one thing the model leaves out and one question you would need answered before using it to describe a real system.
