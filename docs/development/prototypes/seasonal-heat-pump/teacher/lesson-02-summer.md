# Lesson 2: Reverse the Heat Journey

_Draft facilitation plan, 40 minutes._

**Materials:** [student page](../student/lesson-02-summer.md), lesson 1 models, display. **Look for:** correct seasonal directions, accounting for electricity, evidence-based reasoning, and a limitation.

1. **Retrieve (5 min):** Students redraw winter heat and electrical input from memory, then check lesson 1.
2. **Model summer (10 min):** Read the summer card. Students sketch indoor-to-outdoor heat transfer and the separate electrical input; compare with winter.
3. **Account for energy (10 min):** Pairs use the invented values. Winter: 3 outdoor heat units + 1 electrical unit = 4 units delivered indoors. Summer: 3 indoor heat units + 1 electrical unit = 4 units released outdoors. A unit means the same amount of energy in each line. These are illustrative balances, not measured or typical efficiency.
4. **Explain and challenge (10 min):** Students answer the claim and identify an omitted variable. Have partners check the direction of each arrow and whether electricity is included.
5. **Exit (5 min):** Collect the explanation and limitation; give feedback with the [rubric](../assessments/rubric.md).

Provide a sentence frame: “In winter heat moves overall from ___ to ___; the system uses ___.” If the arithmetic distracts, use four equal paper marks and label their sources. The energy accounts omit stored energy and other losses; they assume steady simplified conditions. The model supports no real-home sizing, performance, or purchasing conclusion.
