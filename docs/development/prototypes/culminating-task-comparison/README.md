# Culminating Task Comparison Packet

_Draft Phase 1 review packet for educator review. It is not a selected course assessment or classroom-ready curriculum._

This packet offers three fictional end-task candidates for the developing grades 7–8 Building Science course. Each task lets students use the candidate systems-model outcomes—bound a system, trace an interaction, revise with evidence, and compare a response with tradeoffs and limits—without diagnosing a real building or making a facility recommendation.

## Review Before Use

- All buildings, evidence cards, data, criteria, constraints, and choices are fictional instructional materials. They do not describe the school or a real facility.
- The packet compares candidate assessments; it does not select a culminating task, final outcomes, essential strands, or a nine-week map.
- Do not ask students to inspect the actual school, enter mechanical or restricted spaces, collect health or symptom information, operate equipment, interpret codes, or recommend repairs, purchases, or operating changes.
- Confirm timing, reading level, vocabulary, accessibility supports, and assessment expectations with educators before classroom use.

## Evidence and Source Status

The three candidate tasks are deliberately different ways to make middle-school systems reasoning visible: explanation from supplied evidence, comparison against criteria and constraints, and model revision using supplied data. DOE describes whole-building performance as considering connected consequences for energy, durability, comfort, indoor air quality, safety, cost, aesthetics, and resilience. NGSS middle-school engineering-design expectations include defining criteria and constraints, evaluating competing solutions, and developing models for iterative testing. The National Academies recommends making evidence-based explanations, models, argumentation, and embedded assessment part of investigation and design.

- [DOE Building Science Education: Whole-Building Performance](https://bsesc.energy.gov/training-modules/whole-building-performance)
- [NGSS: MS-ETS1 Engineering Design](https://www.nextgenscience.org/dci-arrangement/ms-ets1-engineering-design)
- [National Academies: Science and Engineering for Grades 6–12](https://nap.nationalacademies.org/resource/25216/interactive/)

All fictional scenarios, tables, diagrams, and prompts are original instructional text. No external visual assets are included.
