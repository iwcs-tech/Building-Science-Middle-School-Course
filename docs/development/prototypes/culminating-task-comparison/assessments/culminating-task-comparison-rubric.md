# Culminating Task Comparison Rubric

_Draft educator-review tool. Use it to compare the evidence generated by the three candidate tasks, not to set a final grading policy._

| Criterion | Beginning | Developing | Secure | Extending |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **System boundary and interaction** | Names parts without a supported relationship. | Identifies a boundary or relationship but leaves a key connection unclear. | Uses supplied evidence to bound a system and trace a plausible interaction. | Explains why a boundary includes some parts and excludes others. |
| **Evidence use** | Gives a preference or unsupported claim. | Uses one supplied fact without connecting it to the claim. | Uses two or more supplied facts, outputs, or observations to support reasoning. | Distinguishes evidence from inference and explains how a new item changes the claim. |
| **Comparison or revision** | Does not compare options or change the first model. | Makes a choice or change with limited connection to criteria or evidence. | Compares options against stated criteria and constraints, or revises a model because of evidence. | Weighs several criteria or explains why the revision is stronger but still provisional. |
| **Tradeoff, limitation, and boundary** | Omits uncertainty or treats the fictional task as a real-building judgment. | Names a limitation or tradeoff without explaining its relevance. | States a relevant tradeoff, limitation, or next question and stays within the fictional boundary. | Explains how missing evidence could qualify the conclusion without overclaiming. |
| **Communication** | Product is incomplete or difficult to follow. | Product has a claim but needs clearer vocabulary or organization. | Product clearly communicates a model or claim, evidence, and qualification. | Product uses precise systems language and responds constructively to an alternative interpretation. |

## Review Notes

- Candidate 1 should make system boundaries and explanation evidence most visible.
- Candidate 2 should make criteria, constraints, tradeoffs, and argument from evidence most visible.
- Candidate 3 should make model revision and the difference between evidence and inference most visible.
- A future final rubric may combine elements only after the course developer selects outcomes and an assessment approach.
