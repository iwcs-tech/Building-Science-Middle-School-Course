# Culminating Task Pacing and Review Matrix

_Developer-facing comparison. This is not a course map._

## Candidate Placement Assumptions

Each candidate assumes students have already encountered the course’s shared systems routine and at least two fictional content contexts. The estimates test feasibility inside a 27-meeting course; they do not allocate the course or choose its strands.

| Candidate | Minimum prior evidence | In-class time | Student product | Review concern |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Explain a bounded system | One system map, one interaction explanation, observation versus inference. | 40–55 minutes | Diagram plus short explanation | May show explanation well but less comparison/revision. |
| Compare whole-building choices | Criteria/constraints practice and at least two content lenses. | 55–70 minutes | Evidence comparison and qualified claim | Needs carefully bounded fictional data to avoid false precision. |
| Revise a model with data | Initial model, inputs/outputs, and a revision routine. | 55–70 minutes | Before-and-after model with evidence note | Students need practice explaining why a revision changes a claim. |

## Candidate Outcome Crosswalk

| Candidate outcome | Explain | Compare | Revise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bound a system | Strong | Moderate | Strong |
| Trace an interaction | Strong | Moderate | Strong |
| Revise with evidence | Moderate | Moderate | Strong |
| Compare a response | Limited | Strong | Moderate |
| State a tradeoff or limitation | Strong | Strong | Strong |

## Educator Review Prompts

- Which product would give the clearest evidence of the outcome that matters most?
- Can students complete the needed reading, modeling, discussion, and writing within the stated time?
- Which vocabulary and representation supports would be necessary for multilingual learners, students using assistive technology, or students who benefit from a partially completed model?
- Does the task remain a fictional evidence-reasoning exercise rather than an actual-school judgment?
- Would one candidate work alone, or would a modest combination better fit the eventual course?

## Decision Boundary

Selecting, combining, scoring, or placing any candidate remains a course-developer and educator decision. A future nine-week map should follow that decision; this packet does not make it.
