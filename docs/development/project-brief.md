## Confirmed Course Setting

- Seventh- and eighth-grade students; approximately 15 students in a typical class
- Nine weeks, three 40-minute meetings each week: 27 meetings and 18 instructional hours
- Standard classroom, not a workshop; teacher laptop, presentation board, and one laptop per student
- At most one field trip assumed
- Markdown is the default format for project artifacts

## Current Design Direction

The course's central idea remains: **buildings are systems whose parts interact with people and the environment.**

Heat flow is being developed as a recurring lens students can use to explain thermal comfort, insulation, sunlight, air movement, and design tradeoffs. It is one important concept among the still-provisional content strands; it does not replace the need to decide how air, moisture, light, materials, sound, energy, climate, and indoor environmental quality will fit together.

See the [Heat-Flow Thread Proposal](heat-flow-thread-proposal.md) and [Moisture Pathways Strand Proposal](moisture-pathways-strand-proposal.md) for the current design work.
